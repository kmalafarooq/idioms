Top 10 New Idioms

These are some idioms i have noticed all over the internet and the ones i love the most. :)

  1. FOMO : Fear Of Missing Out
  2. Touch grass : Take a break from the internet and spend time in the real world.
  3. It's giving ... : It has the look, feel, or impression of a very specific thing.
  4. Living rent-free : Staying on someone's mind without effort.
  5. Fork Found in kitchen : Response to someone pointing out something that is very obvious.
  6. Make it make sense : Explain something that seems illogical.
  7. Understood the assignment : Did exactly what was needed
  8. Let them cook : Give someone time to show what they can do.
  9. For the plot : doing somthing simply for the sake of making life more interesting
  10. Spill the tea : To Share Juicy Gossip
